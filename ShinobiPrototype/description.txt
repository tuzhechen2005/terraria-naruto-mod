Naruto: Shinobi Path is a Naruto fan mod for Terraria. Start in the Hidden Leaf Village, travel to the Land of Waves with Kakashi, and face Zabuza and Haku before returning for the Chunin Exams. Chakra, substitution, and style cores add ninja techniques alongside Terraria's vanilla classes and bosses. Start a new world to generate the village, bridge, and exam sites. The mod is still in development.
