Produce ONE transparent PNG canvas with a spacious, strict 2 by 2 arrangement of FOUR isolated effects, generous empty transparent gutters between and around all effects, no touching or overlap. No panels, gridlines, labels, letters, ground, characters, swords, scenery, or shadows. Top left: upright menacing purple demon-shaped chakra specter, clear readable oni-like face with horns/brow/eyes, broad shoulders and raised smoky flame silhouette, lower torso dissolving into translucent violet smoke; ethereal decorative aura with no solid body. Top right: THE EXACT SAME demon face, horns, eyes, shoulder structure and palette, now stretched forward and downward in a crushing heavy-slash pose, lower body fading into smoke; maintain the same bottom anchor height as top left. Bottom left: a broad purple rotational sword motion arc, clearly OPEN rather than a closed circle, bright leading edge, softer fading trailing edge, readable clockwise energy motion with no physical blade. Bottom right: purple chakra impact ring with a completely clear transparent center, thin luminous perimeter and outward breaking smoky flame shards, no filled orb. Style: polished hand-painted anime game VFX against genuine transparent alpha, controlled purple/violet/indigo/lavender palette, distinct silhouettes, smooth semi-transparent gradients, soft edge glow but no giant bloom. Each effect centered in its quadrant and fills most of it while retaining empty margins. Ghost pair must obviously be two poses of the same face. All four are decorative overlays, not independent attackers. No text, no watermark.

The copied source sheet has only its fully transparent pixels' hidden RGB cleared; visible RGBA pixels are unchanged. `python3 export.py` recreates the four game PNGs and previews from this sheet.
